- Tone preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: Dramatic Makoto Shinkai anime movie trailer — romantic, ethereal, breathtaking scale
- Interpretation: Deep indigo backgrounds, glowing sakura and cyan neon accents, deliberate hold times for typography readability, and seamless scene transitions.
- Angle: A cinematic love letter platform combining modern WebGL and emotional Japanese storytelling.
- Hook: Ethereal midnight title card emerging into the Tokyo starlight.
- Outro / punchline: "Zero 3D models. Zero audio files. 100% procedural love."
