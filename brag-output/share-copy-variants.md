### LinkedIn (Engineering & Craft)
I built Koistory: an interactive 3D anime love letter experience inspired by the films of Makoto Shinkai.

What makes it unique:
- **Zero Asset Overhead**: The celestial moon, Tokyo skyline, cherry blossom trees, and silhouetted couple are 100% procedurally synthesized in client WebGL.
- **Dynamic Weather Systems**: Instanced particle systems switch seamlessly between Sakura Night (350 falling petals), Tokyo Rain (750 high-velocity rain streaks with deck splash), and Golden Dawn (220 shimmering morning motes).
- **Interactive Storyflow**: Recipients break a physical-style wax seal gate to read personalized vows along a continuous C1 spline camera plunge into Tokyo.
- **Procedural Audio**: Real-time Web Audio synth with resonant pink noise wind and pentatonic chime delay networks.

Live deployment on Vercel: https://koistory.vercel.app
