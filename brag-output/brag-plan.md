# Brag Plan: Koistory (恋の物語)

## What is this app?
Koistory is an interactive, cinematic 3D anime love letter platform where users compose and seal heartfelt memoirs beneath the Tokyo midnight moon, featuring procedural Three.js graphics, dynamic weather themes, GSAP scroll camera trajectories, and real-time Web Audio.

## The angle
Anime movie romance brought to the modern web. Instead of sending a static text message or generic e-card, send a cinematic 3D Tokyo rooftop experience where breaking an encrypted wax seal unveils your vow suspended in starlight over the Tokyo skyline—all 100% procedurally synthesized without a single downloaded 3D model or audio file.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dramatic midnight title card emerges from the darkness with glowing Japanese kanji (`恋の物語`) and celestial starlight, immediately establishing the Makoto Shinkai anime aesthetic before revealing the Tokyo rooftop silhouette.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Rooftop Summit**: Silhouetted couple leaning against the railing, falling sakura petals, and the procedural FBM moon halo.
- **The Wax Seal Ritual**: Interactive glowing wax seal gate with kanji stamp (`絆`) breaking open to reveal the recipient's personal letter.
- **Dynamic Weather Shift**: Instant transformation between Sakura Night (pink petals), Tokyo Rain (high-velocity neon rain streaks), and Golden Dawn (warm floating sunrise motes).
- **The Streetscape Plunge**: Smooth GSAP camera dive soaring over the railing toward the distant illuminated Tokyo skyscrapers with floating sky lanterns.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero 3D models. Zero audio files. 100% procedural love." A final glowing starlight reveal with the Koistory logo and shareable link.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (Recipient receives secret link `koistory.vercel.app/m/...`) → Key Action (Tap to break the wax seal with audio chime) → Result (Camera plunges into the customized 3D rooftop memoir with floating sky lanterns).
